# Visiting Hours

A hardware wallet that will not sign a transaction at 3 AM — not because it is
tired, but because it has become a paranoid institution. Visiting hours are
9 to 5, and at 3:17 AM it looks at its own tamper-evident seal and no longer
trusts even that: the one part of itself meant to prove nobody has opened it.
So it refuses, flatly, until daylight.

> Visiting hours are 9-5. It's 3:17 AM, and frankly, I suspect my own tamper-evident seal. No sign.
